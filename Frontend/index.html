About Gojo Rentals

Gojo Rentals is your premier partner for finding high-quality luxury and comfortable rental properties in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Whether you're looking for a modern apartment in Bole, a serene villa in Old Airport, or a charming guesthouse in Kazanchis, we curate the best options for your short-term or long-term stay. Our mission is to make finding your perfect temporary home in Addis simple, secure, and satisfying.

Founded by enthusiasts passionate about Ethiopian hospitality and real estate, we strive to provide exceptional service and connect renters with reliable hosts. Explore our listings and experience the best of Addis Ababa living!
